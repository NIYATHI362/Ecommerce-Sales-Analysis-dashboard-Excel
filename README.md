# Ecommerce-Sales-Analysis-dashboard-Excel
An interactive US E-commerce Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product popularity, demographics, geography, delivery time, and customer satisfaction. It uses Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to turn raw e-commerce data into an interactive, easy-to-understand business dashboard.
#Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Orders: 2,400
Total Quantity: 11,997
Total Amount: $649.0K
Average Rating: 4.0
Average Days to Deliver: 2.3 days

# Business Questions Answered
The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

What is the sales and quantity trend over the last 13 weeks?
How do customers prefer to purchase?
How many products do customers typically buy?
Which products are most popular?
What is the overall gender distribution of customers?
Where are the customers located?
How long does it take to ship orders?
How satisfied are the customers?

# Dashboard Features
# 1. Sales Trend Analysis
Analyzes quantity and sales amount trends across the last 13 weeks to identify changes in overall sales performance.
# 2. Customer Purchase Behavior
Shows how many items customers purchase per order, helping understand customer buying patterns.
# 3. Order Mode Analysis
Analyzes customer purchasing channels such as:
App
Instagram
Partner App
Target.com
Website
# 4. Product Analysis
Highlights popular products and provides a gender-wise breakdown of product purchases.
# 5. Customer Demographics
Visualizes the distribution of customers across:
Female
Male
Other
Unknown
# 6. Geographical Analysis
Displays the geographical distribution of customers and quantity purchased across different locations in the United States.
# 7. Delivery Analysis
Analyzes the number of days taken to ship customer orders.
# 8. Customer Satisfaction
Visualizes customer ratings/satisfaction across different months and customer segments.
